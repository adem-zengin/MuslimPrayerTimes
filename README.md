## ✨ Features

- **Official Prayer Times:** Accurate prayer schedules sourced directly from the Turkish Directorate of Religious Affairs (Diyanet)

- **Hijri Calendar:** View and track Islamic Hijri calendar dates seamlessly

- **Smart Notifications:** Timely alerts for prayer times and customizable pre-prayer reminders to help you prepare in advance

- **Automatic Silent Mode:** Automatically mutes your device during prayer times to ensure uninterrupted worship

- **Headless Background Service:** All notifications, reminders, and automatic muting features run reliably in the background without needing to keep the app open

- **Full Offline Functionality:** Downloads 30 days of data at once, allowing the app to operate completely offline for a full month

- **Multilingual Interface:** Full language support for English, Arabic, and Turkish

---

## 📋 Requirements

- BlackBerry 10 device (rooted)
